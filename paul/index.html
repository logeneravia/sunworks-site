Paul (Chip) Harris, owner of Sunworks

What drives me

Sunworks is a hobby that got away from me. I love it: the people who work here, the artists on our walls, the neighbours who come in for lunch. But the shop isn’t what gets me up in the morning. What drives me is a question: who are we becoming, and who are we becoming together?

I’ve spent most of my life working on that question: in business, on Red Deer City Council, in the arts and in community work, and through study in Canada, the United States and Europe. Here’s where I’ve landed. We don’t become ourselves in isolation. Who we are takes shape in our relationships with the people we spend time with, the places we live, the things we cherish, the ideas we wrestle with, and the things we do. Those relationships carry us, often without our noticing. But we’re never without the power to change them, and in changing them, to change ourselves.

I call this Relational Identity Theory, and everything I’m building grows out of it.

Part futurist, part philosopher, part pragmatist. Travelling companions are essential. If any of this resonates, come in, have a coffee, and let’s talk.

What I’m building

The podcast

Good Conversations with Paul

The theory in practice. I pick guests I want to change me, and we talk about who they’re becoming. Every conversation changes the future in some way.

Coaching

Reflexive Practice

My coaching and mentoring work, helping people and organisations find paths to their ideal identity and life.

The app

Logeneravia

The path of conscious becoming. A living map of the relationships shaping your life, so you can see what supports you and choose where to go next.

For small businesses

Strumworks

Text messages and loyalty for local Canadian businesses, built here in Red Deer. It sends the Sunworks lunch texts, so regulars hear what’s on before they come in.
