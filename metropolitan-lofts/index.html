Long-term leases, six months or longer

We’re looking for long-term tenants. The lofts are fully furnished apartments for professionals who want a home base downtown, with the shops, restaurants and the rest of Red Deer right outside the door.

Inside the lofts

Furnished accommodation in Red Deer

  • A full kitchen and a sitting area, furnished and ready to use
  • Heated floors, granite counters, 12-foot ceilings and skylights
  • A queen bed, for up to two guests
  • Wireless internet and a washing machine
  • Second floor of a rebuilt heritage building, reached by stairs
  • Street parking, with paid lots nearby

Layouts differ from loft to loft. Loft 201 has an office and two walkout balconies, and Loft 203 is a studio. Each loft’s page lists its own amenities.

Restaurants, cafés, parks and trails are within walking distance, including City Hall Park and the city bus terminal. Professional guests are preferred, and we ask for ID at check-in.
