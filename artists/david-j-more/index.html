Artist: David J. More

Selected work from the White Gallery.

Art on Display in Our White Gallery

David More’s paintings and mixed media drawings are in many private, corporate, and institutional collections in Canada, the United States, and abroad. In 1993, the University of Calgary presented one of his paintings to Mikhail Gorbachev, the former President of the Soviet Union. We’re happy to have him featured as well in the White Gallery at Sunworks Living / Studios.
