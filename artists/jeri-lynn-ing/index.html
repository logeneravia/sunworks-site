About the artist

Jeri-Lynn’s vibrant works reflect her deep connection to place and spirit, in a contemporary style. Her practice spans painting, printmaking, and mixed media, often drawing on themes of growth and renewal. We’re delighted to feature her artistry in the White Gallery.
