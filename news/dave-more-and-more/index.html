Letters from Sunworks · August 28, 2026

Dave More and more...

Three pieces never seen before · Opening Sunday · Good Conversations with Paul, Episode 8 at 2

This Sunday belongs to Dave More. We're hanging new work of his in the gallery — including three pieces that have never been seen before, anywhere. We are so proud. They go on the wall here first. The gallery opens at 10 a.m., and the three new pieces will hang veiled — on the wall, under cover, keeping their secret — until the reveal at 3 p.m. Every other artist's work — the whole rest of the show — is yours to wander from ten o'clock on. If you know Dave's work, you know why that sentence matters; if you don't, this is the weekend to find out.

At the heart of the opening, the chairs come out. We'll film Episode 8 of Good Conversations with Paul: Dave and I sit down in front of his work, and go where this series loves to go — belonging, becoming, and what can shape a life when you pay attention to it. An artist in conversation, surrounded by paintings that no one — except Dave and his curators — has seen until this weekend. That doesn't happen often in any city.

Our conversation begins Sunday at 2:00 p.m. sharp — please be seated by 1:53 — the doors shut, the camera rolls at two, and there's no late seating. Free, as always. Bring your curiosity. If you miss it, you can catch the conversation on my YouTube channel: youtube.com/@reflexivepractice

Sunday, at a glance:

10:00 a.m. — The gallery opens; the whole show on view. Dave's three new pieces hang veiled. 1:53 p.m. — Be seated. The doors shut at two. 2:00 p.m. — Good Conversations with Paul, Episode 8: Dave More. 3:00 p.m. — The unveiling.

Also new on the walls: Jeri Lyn arrives with all-new pieces — local scenes in oil, plein air landscapes and garden paintings, the kind of realism our clients adore.

And... a first showing in the White Gallery for a brand-new artist, Jessie Emilie — an oil painter out of the Alberta University of the Arts, now in her Master's at UBC Okanagan, who paints the figure with unapologetic honesty. First shows matter. Come be part of her first yes here.

As ever Paul and the team of Sunworkers.

Two new faces behind the counter this month: Stephanie and Ryker have joined the Sunworkers, and they're already part of the family. Come in, say hello, and let them learn your name — being known here is rather the point of the place.
