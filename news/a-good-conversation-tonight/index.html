Letters from Sunworks · November 19, 2026

A good conversation tonight

Part 1 of 5 · Not a massive missive.

There is so much to share, but no massive missive today. This is part one of five short ones. I am practising restraint, I won't blast you with the verbal fireshose. You may wish to keep this as evidence. Ha!

I've been so busy since returning from Italy. Loraine and I have been ordering all of the new stock that I haven't had time to send you any updates except by SMS. The new and old crew have been ploughing through the shipments. I counted this morning and we've had 24 shipments.

For the pantry: bucatini, orecchiette, fregola and more pasta shapes, basil pesto, ginger beer and Sicilian blood orange soda. Lakrids advent calendars, assorted Danish liquorice, hot chocolate, English preserves, fudge and almonds caramelized with honey. Canadian-made handcrafted toffee in maple pecan, holiday spice and almond espresso. Chai honey and chai concentrate. Raw creamed honey in cinnamon, lemon ginger and hot chili pepper.

For the house: hobnail glass tumblers, whiskey glasses, and jugs in clear, royal blue and black smoke. Cast iron casseroles, roasters and frypans in blue, green and merlot. Modern Cuckoo clocks, raku-fired crackle sheep and cast iron crows. Lothantique soaps, hand creams and diffusers, and genuine Marseille olive oil soap.

To wear, carry and give: leather bags and leather journals, Shupatto folding bags, steel rings and bracelets, Moonglow necklaces, Sibilia earrings and pendants,Parisian sunglasses and reading glasses, Austrain eau de parfum, and slim card-protector wallets. And for the long evenings: new Paperblanks journals and bookmarks, Leuchtturm1917 journals and pens, books (Disappointing Affirmations among them), and Printworks chess, backgammon, dominoes and 1000-piece puzzles.

Tonight, Thursday, November 19, I sit down with Janice Wing for Good Conversations with Paul, beginning at 6 p.m. sharp.

Janice brings a life of experience in education, public service and community leadership. That gives us plenty to talk about, but what interests me most is the person behind all that doing. The people, places and relationships that have shaped her. Who she is becoming, and what she is paying attention to now.

That is why I love these conversations. We come together, listen, and sometimes leave with questions we hadn't thought to ask ourselves.

I hope you'll join us at Sunworks this evening. Come a little early so you're settled in for our six o'clock start.

There are paintings, Christmas discoveries and a little fundraising news to share in the next instalments. One thing at a time. See? Restraint.
