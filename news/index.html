A little time with the walls

There is something about seeing a painting in person: the colour, the texture, the way it changes as you step back. A photograph primes the imagination. Standing in front of a painting is another thing entirely. And speaking of smiling.

Three paintings by Dave from the Robert and Maryann Ness collection are also hanging in the White Gallery. They offer a way to support something close to home.

November 19, 2026

A good conversation tonight

Janice brings a life of experience in education, public service and community leadership. That gives us plenty to talk about, but what interests me most is the person behind all that doing. The people, places and relationships that have shaped her. Who she is becoming, and what she is paying attention to now.

That is why I love these conversations. We come together, listen, and sometimes leave with questions we hadn't thought to ask ourselves.

September 19, 2026

Fall, Balance, and Becoming

One story from my conversation with Dave More has stayed with me ever since we talked. On July 14, 2000, Dave was painting a towering summer cloud near Benalto. For reasons he still can't explain, he reached into his paint box, loaded his brush with deep red, and swept it through the middle of the cloud.

The colour troubled him, but he left it. Later that evening he learned that the very cloud he'd been painting all afternoon had drifted southeast and become the Pine Lake tornado.

August 30, 2026

Tomorrow.

A little of their history, in Dave's own words. The three are Twilight at Parlee, New Brunswick (2020), Last Sail at Parlee (2021), and Passage — Bay of Chaleur, which he finished only this past spring; parts of its surface were still soft in August.

The first two began on a September evening in 2014, when Dave and Yvette climbed the dunes above Parlee Beach to watch the sun settle. The sea took on a mantle of jade; the retreating tide left pools where the shadows glowed crimson and orange.

August 28, 2026

Dave More and more...

At the heart of the opening, the chairs come out. We'll film Episode 8 of Good Conversations with Paul: Dave and I sit down in front of his work, and go where this series loves to go — belonging, becoming, and what can shape a life when you pay attention to it.

An artist in conversation, surrounded by paintings that no one — except Dave and his curators — has seen until this weekend. That doesn't happen often in any city.
