To home, with love and pasta

Back from Genova · Pasta Take-Away Wednesday · a new card line · Good Conversations Oct 15 · dates to remember

Pasta Take-Away · this Wednesday from 11 a.m. Gemelli al pesto, Crema di funghi, or Bolognese — $9 each with grissini. Order ahead: text PASTA to 431-340-3455 with your choice, how many, and a pick-up time.

Ciao from Red Deer!

I'm home from Italy: inspired, relaxed, refreshed... and so very jet lagged. My body landed on Saturday. Terry picked me up and dragged me home. The rest of me is still somewhere over the Atlantic and expected any day now.

I spent most of my trip in Genova, I stayed in a place that overlooked Piazza Fossatello, a little square that has been there for more than 800 years. It takes its name from a small stream that still runs underneath it. In the 1300s it had public baths, and later, for over three centuries, it was the city's fruit and vegetable market. Every day I wandered the caruggi, the narrow lanes of the old city. Genova's historic centre is one of the largest medieval quarters in Europe, and you could walk it for a month and still turn a corner you've never seen. My last day there I took a day trip down the coast to Chiavari to see my friends there. Never miss an opportunity for a good train ride.

On the way home, I spent two nights in Torino.

And in between, I wrote. I'm working on fictional/experimental short story which explores identity, healing, and change. As you may know my work is about how we become who we are through the people, places and practices around us, and what it really takes to become someone new. I'm really proud of this work, but gosh it's got a long way to go.

I also brought something home you can eat. Anywhere I could find pesto it had it. In fact there is risk of my turning green. In Genova, there is an entire fleet of vans dedicated to delivering basil.

Pesto pasta to go, the Genovese way. This is where the idea came from.

This Wednesday we're trying Pasta Take-Away. Gemelli al pesto, Crema di funghi, or Bolognese, $9 each with grissini, ready for pick-up from 11 a.m. Loraine and I worked together on Sunday and made a plan for Wednesday, and today we're already taking orders.

To order ahead, text PASTA to 431-340-3455 with your choice, how many, and a pick-up time. We'll text you back to confirm, and have it ready for you.

Meanwhile, the shop has been taken over. Blaine is back from India and busy fixing the scars in our shop floors, and the rest of us have been banished until Wednesday. We can't wait to see you when the doors open.

Behind the scenes, well actually right in the middle of everything, Loraine, Michelle and I are on an ordering frenzy this week. So many new things are on their way, plus a few favourites we'd run out of. Watch the shelves. It's all coming in fast a furious over the next six weeks. That'll teach me for skipping the Paris buying trip.

And, here's some news I'm excited about. I've created a new line of greeting cards, and they'll hit our shelves in the next couple of weeks.

Meet Spindle and Bubba: two aliens observing humanity, trying very hard to make sense of how ridiculous we can be. The series began years ago as pencil sketches in my notebook. Now they're cards for birthdays, new homes, thinking-of-you moments and more, with the humour and the greeting on the front.

Later this month, Good Conversations returns Thursday, October 15. I'm hoping to confirm our guest this week, so stay tuned.

If you haven't been in lately, the White Gallery walls are full. Dave More's New Brunswick seascapes are here, including a piece that had never been exhibited before, and a couple of them have already found new homes. More new work from Dave is on its way. Jeri Lyn Ing's local scenes and plein air landscapes in oil have been loved: three have sold already. And Jessie Emilie's honest, beautiful figure paintings are still up, her first showing with us. Come and see them while they're still on the walls.

We're two weeks into autumn, and there are just 81 sleeps until Christmas. (Yes, we counted.)

Every conversation changes the future, especially if it comes with pasta. I'm glad to be home, and I hope to see you this week.

Paul and the team of Sunworkers.
