Letters from Sunworks · July 23, 2026

Things are changing

Hello and goodbye

The summer quiet July gave us a gift: time to fall back in love with our own store. We've spent the past week remerchandising nearly every corner — the front window, the hallway, the shelves you thought you knew. Honestly? The store has never looked better. Come wander it slowly and see what's new.

A change worth marking in your calendar: starting July 27/28, we're adjusting our hours — Wednesday-Saturday 8:30 - 5:30 and Sunday 10:00 - 4:00; CLOSED Mondays and Tuesdays. It lets us pour our best energy into the days you're actually out and about.

The store hours are bit wonky at the end of the month so note this as well. We're closed from July 31 - August 4. A nice long weekend.

Sad news for us here: Janet is leaving us at the end of the month. Drop by on the 30th to say your good byes. I guess maybe we'll have a cake for her.

Which means we are looking for new part time staff. If you know someone, please free free to pass on this newsletter.

Meanwhile, think about your garden. The flowers come and go. The stone ones stay to keep watch. Concrete statuary, cast in Canada — come find your garden's quiet companion.

Thank you for being the kind of community that small shops appreciate.

— Paul & the Sunworkers

PS: One small housekeeping note: this letter arrives from our own new newsletter system — built right here at Sunworks, on an Italian working retreat. This replaces the big mailing service we'd used for years. Same us, hopefully better letters. If this found its way to you and it's not your cup of tea, truly no hard feelings — the unsubscribe link at the very bottom works with one click, and we'll never trouble your inbox again. And if you stay: thank you. Having you here matters more than you know.
