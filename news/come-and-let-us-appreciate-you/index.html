Letters from Sunworks · November 25, 2026

Come and let us appreciate you

Sip & Shop · Friday, November 27 · 4–8 p.m.

You make Sunworks much more than a shop. You bring the conversations, the laughter and the reasons we love coming to work every day. This Friday evening, we’d like to say thank you.

Join us for baking, hot olives wine and cheese, apple cider, and giggle water, a little holiday cheer and an unhurried wander through the shop. Bring a sister, bring a friend, or come on your own—everyone is welcome.

There are lovely new arrivals to discover, Dave More’s paintings to enjoy in the gallery, and gifts for the people on your list. We’ll be here to help you find something that feels just right.

A little advent-calendar appreciation: on Friday evening, receive 30% off Lakrids advent calendars with a purchase of $100 or more. We have 25 calendars left, and the offer lasts only while they do.

And you could win your purchase back—up to $200! Shop during Sip & Shop and enter our customer-appreciation draw. Your Christmas shopping could end up being our treat.

Come for the shopping. Stay for the company. Most of all, come and let us appreciate you.

See you Friday,

Paul, Terry, Loraine, Michelle, Blaine, Stephenie, Haythem, and Sharman.
