Fall, Balance, and Becoming

The equinox issue · Good Conversations · a cloud, a red brushstroke, and a phone call · fall & winter hours

Open Sunday September 20, 2026. 10-4 AND.....Tuesday is the fall equinox.

It's one of my favourite days of the year because. The day and night are perfectly balanced.Oh, that we all could be balanced in the same way. The equinox seems like a good day to pause and ask ourselves what the next season could be all about.

I've been thinking a lot about becoming and really framing this time of the year about a transitions between working hard all summer to celebrating and appreciating our accomplishments and our 'becomings' What does it mean becoming... ourselves?

That's really what Good Conversations is all about.

Every month I sit down with someone whom I wouldn't mind being changed by, whom I trust, whom I respect. We don't spend much time talking about careers or résumés or our expertise. Instead, we ask different questions about who we are and how we become who we are... and who we might become through our choices and relationships.

Who are you becoming? It's a surprisingly complex question to delve into. And it's fascinating to discover that nearly everyone answers it by talking about relationships. The people who believed in them. The places that changed them. The ideas that captured their imagination. The work that shaped their character.

One story from my conversation with Dave More has stayed with me ever since we talked.

On July 14, 2000, Dave was painting a towering summer cloud near Benalto. For reasons he still can't explain, he reached into his paint box, loaded his brush with deep red, and swept it through the middle of the cloud. He never paints clouds that way he tells us.

The colour troubled him, but he left it.

Later that evening he learned that the very cloud he'd been painting all afternoon had drifted southeast and become the Pine Lake tornado. Dave has never sold that painting. He donated it to the Red Deer Museum and Art Gallery (MAG) because, in his words, it had become part of a much larger story.

Whether you see it as intuition, coincidence, or simply one of life's mysteries, it's one of those stories that reminds us there is still much we don't understand about creativity, memory, and paying attention. Nor how the future is formed, and affects the present.

Dave's artwork is now on our gallery walls—including a few paintings that have never been exhibited before. Watch Good Conversations Episode 8 with Dave More:

A few weeks earlier I sat down with local artist Dawn Candy. Somehow she never got the newsletter she deserved. She talked about discovering, halfway through life, that she needs to live near water. She doesn't know exactly what that means yet, only that it's true. She talked about loving magpies because, as she says, "They're like crows that don't take themselves quite so seriously."

And she shared the story of a quiet student who handed her a letter after an eight-week class—one of those moments that reminds us we rarely know the influence we're having on another person's life.

Like all the best conversations, it wasn't really about pottery. It was about paying attention to who we're becoming.
