A little time with the walls

Part 2 of 5 · Dave More · Smiles Thru Lindsey

Today for part 2 of 5 let's look at the walls. With the new products in the shop, the walls are full and happy. Advent calendars have arrived, cuckoo clocks, disappointing affirmation calendars... it's time to get your holiday or Christmas shopping underway.

BUT... It's the gallery walls that I really want you to stop by and have a look at. We've received some new Dave More paintings. These are the largest we've ever show and they are stunning. If you've got a big wall in your office or home that is crying out for love, stop by and see if one of these beauties will make you smile every time you pass.

There is something about seeing a painting in person: the colour, the texture, the way it changes as you step back. A photograph primes the imagination. Standing in front of a painting is another thing entirely. And speaking of smiling.

Three paintings by Dave from the Robert and Maryann Ness collection are also hanging in the White Gallery. They offer a way to support something close to home.

Smiles Thru Lindsey was established by Rick and Cindy More in memory of their daughter Lindsey, carrying forward her wish to help young people access mental-health support. The foundation supports youth mental-health initiatives in our community, and the commission from these paintings will go to that work.

Come to check out our walls in the shop and the White Gallery. Find some great gifts or a painting that makes you pause your life to enjoy. I think that’s a pretty good reason to come downtown.
