Giving back

Community

Sunworks is part of Red Deer, and some of what we care about should outlast any one season in the shop. That is why we hold two funds at the Community Foundation of Central Alberta.

Two funds that keep giving

The Community Foundation of Central Alberta, formerly the Red Deer & District Community Foundation, has looked after gifts for our region since 1989. It holds both funds, makes the grants and issues tax receipts. Anyone can give to either one, in any amount.

Environment

Sunworks Environment Legacy Fund

Sunworks’ own fund for the environment. It provides grants to organizations working in the field of the environment.

Give to the Environment Legacy Fund

Equality and inclusion

Stonewall Community Fund

We started this fund in 1993 and named it for the 1969 Stonewall uprising. It supports mental health in the LGBTQ2S+ community and works to end gender discrimination through education and community involvement, work that is still needed today. The fund is also building toward a multi-purpose community centre.

In 2019 Paul, Terry and Sunworks gave the fund new life with the 52 for 52 campaign.

Give to the Stonewall Fund

Ways to give
